# Story flow

Flow is the outline board: **acts → beats → chapters**.

## Beats
A beat is a title, a summary and a status (`planned`, `written`, `cut`). Move beats between acts, reorder them, and link one to a chapter when it becomes prose.

## Working with the model
The story agent can propose a beat sheet, expand one beat into a scene plan, or check that every act has a turn. Proposed plans live in `story_plans` until you accept them, so a bad suggestion never edits your outline.

## Practical loop
1. Beats for the current act only — future acts stay fuzzy.
2. Expand the next beat into a chapter when you sit down to write.
3. Mark the beat written once the chapter exists.
