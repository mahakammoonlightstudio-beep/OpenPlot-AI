# Writing stats

Stats counts words you actually wrote, per day and per project. It is local and never uploaded.

## What counts
- Edits to chapter content, chat replies you keep, and story entries.
- Removed words count negatively, so copy-paste and deletion do not inflate the graph.
- Only deltas are recorded — rewriting the same sentence five times adds no words.

## Reading the graph
- **Today** resets at local midnight.
- **Streak** counts consecutive days with any positive delta.
- **Per project** shows where the writing went.

The numbers are approximate by design: this is a motivational graph, not an audit trail.
