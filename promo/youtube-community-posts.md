## Post utama (English)

> I got so bored of writing stories across five browser tabs and a stray .txt file that I built my own writing platform. And it's free.
>
> OpenPlot AI is a desktop story studio for Windows, macOS and Linux:
>
> • AI chat with any provider — OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or fully local via Ollama and LM Studio
> • Story bible: worlds, characters, lore, all linked and searchable
> • Chapters with focus mode and version history
> • Three-act Story Flow board
> • Writing streaks and stats that keep you honest
>
> No account, no cloud, no telemetry — your data stays in a local SQLite database. Open source, MIT. The macOS dmg is a universal binary: one download for Intel and Apple silicon.
>
> Free download (auto-detects your OS): https://mahakammoonlightstudio-beep.github.io/OpenPlot-AI/
